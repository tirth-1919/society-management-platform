Help & Support

Quick answers for using your resident portal.

How do I pay a bill?

Open My Bills, select a pending bill, choose a payment method, and confirm your payment.

What does Pending mean?

Pending means there is an unpaid balance on the bill. Partially Paid means some, but not all, of the bill has been paid. Overdue means the due date has passed and a late fee applies.

What is the late fee?

A one-time late fee is added to a bill once it passes its due date without being paid in full. It appears as a separate line on the bill and does not increase again for the same month.

How do I download or print a receipt?

Open My Receipts. Choose Download to save the PDF, or Print to open it in your browser's PDF viewer and print from there.

Login help

Use your registered mobile number and password to sign in. If you've forgotten your password, contact the society office to reset it — residents don't currently have a self-service reset.

Registration help

New residents register through the sign-up form and select their wing, block, and flat. Your account becomes active once the society office reviews and approves the request.

Still need help?

Submit a support request and the society office will get back to you.
